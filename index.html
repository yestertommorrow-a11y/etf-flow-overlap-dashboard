ETF Flow & Overlap Dashboard

Fund flows · 1-vs-1 overlap · sector weights · correlation · holdings change — all from free TickerInside data. Browse every fund of any issuer · Invesco funds get holdings from SEC Form N-PORT and weight changes from quarterly reports.

⚠ Upfront: no flow series for Invesco-related funds — flows exist only for ETFs read daily from iShares, SPDR or Global X files (etc. QQQ is Invesco, so it has none). SEC-filed funds (Invesco, Vanguard…) publish nothing daily. For those use the Holdings Change tab, where Invesco works from quarterly SEC reports.

Filters the covered universe server-side (/screen, no key), so any issuer works — type iShares, Vanguard, SPDR, Global X… Pick buttons fill the other tabs: A/B set the compare tabs, ＋ appends to Correlation and Holdings Change.

Data: TickerInside (CC BY 4.0), issuer holdings history and SEC Form N-PORT filings. Overlap = Σ min(wᵢᴀ, wᵢʙ) over shared holdings.
